      Questions

      What am I actually buying?

      The exclusive right to inscribe up to 108 characters onto one specific minute of the future, in UTC time. When that minute arrives, your words hold this page, live, for its entire sixty seconds, then rest permanently in the Ledger. One minute, one owner, forever.

      Is this a token or an NFT?

      No. There is no coin and no smart contract to buy. Your minute is written into a public hash-chain: each engraving cryptographically seals the one before it, so no entry can ever be altered without breaking every seal after it. The chain is periodically anchored into the Bitcoin blockchain, so its existence at a point in time is provable independently of this website.

      How do I pay?

      In Bitcoin. When you claim a minute you are shown the exact address and the exact amount. The first payment confirmed on the Bitcoin network to that address wins the minute.

      When are my words shown?

      Never before the minute arrives. Words engraved on a future minute stay sealed, not displayed here or anywhere, until their moment comes. Then they appear live for sixty seconds, and afterward permanently in the Ledger.

      Can I change or delete my words later?

      No. Once engraved, they are permanent. Choose them the way you would choose your last.

      Can I buy a minute that already passed?

      No. The past cannot be bought. Only a minute still to come can bear your words.

      Can I earn from sharing?

      Yes. Any Bitcoin address is your referral link. No signup, no purchase required. Every minute sold through your link pays you 25% in Bitcoin, and you earn up to 10% more on the sales of the network you bring in, four levels deep. It rewards real sales only, and every payout is published with its Bitcoin transaction id on the Affiliate Ledger.

      What if this website disappears?

      Every visitor can download the whole monument: a single self-contained file that carries the entire chain inside it and verifies itself in your browser, offline. Anyone can re-publish it. The Ledger does not depend on this domain.
